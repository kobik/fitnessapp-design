How C behaves

  • Two views: the list (everything closed) or one open exercise that fills the space between the header and the log bar. The open one is the one the bar logs.
  • Entering: the first exercise is already open. There's no Start button. The clock reads "Not started" until the first set is logged.
  • Back to the list: "All exercises" at the top, a tap on the title, or Escape. The view shrinks back into its row. In the list, tapping a row grows it into the open view.
  • Next and previous: ‹ › beside "3 of 6", or a sideways swipe. The bar follows.
  • After an exercise's last set, the next unfinished exercise slides in and "3 of 6" updates. After the very last set, the list shows every result.
  • The list: rows never move. Each shows ✓ and its result, "2 of 3 sets", or the target ("3 × 5 · 75 kg"). There are no log or tick buttons on rows, and the bar shows the rest timer and "Open an exercise to log".
  • Rest belongs to the workout. It starts after each set and keeps running when you switch.
  • Inside the open exercise: tap a logged set to fix its numbers in place. Tap its check to un-log it, with Undo in the message. ⋯ has warm-up, remove set, swap, note, history and remove exercise. Swap and remove ask first if sets are logged.
  • Workout level: Finish (outlined until every set is logged, then the one solid button in the bar), Workout options (add exercise, reorder, session note, discard), and ⌄ to minimize. Discard always asks.
  • Motion is 300 ms or less and eases out. With reduced motion turned on, every change is instant.

  • The tab bar hides while you train. The bottom of the screen is the only place a thumb reaches easily, and logging needs all of it. The tab bar comes back with GO as the white live timer, and tapping it brings you back (try "Left the screen").
  • White means live. The clock, the progress bar, the Log button, the logged checks and the rest bar are white. Lime appears only as the dot before "Push A". A freestyle workout has no dot.
  • One solid button. Log is the only solid button. Finish stays outlined in the header until every set is logged. Then Finish moves down into Log's place, and the header copy goes away so it isn't shown twice.
  • The weight is already suggested, with the reason. Each set starts with what progression picked, plus the app's own reason line ("Every rep last time. 2.5 kg more.") and the rule behind it. If you change the weight, it carries to the sets after it that aren't logged yet.
  • Rest starts when you log a set and uses that exercise's own time: 2:00 for the barbell presses, 1:30 for the dumbbell press and dips, 1:00 for raises and pushdowns. You can take off or add 15 seconds, or Skip. At zero it says Ready.
  • No history yet means no weight is suggested. The weight shows "–", and Log asks for a weight first (a barbell starts on the bar).
  • Discard is never one tap. Finish → Discard workout → "Discard workout? The 8 sets you logged in this session will be lost." Removing an exercise and swapping one that has logged sets also ask first.
  • Long names drop to a smaller size and stop at two lines. The full name is in the ⋯ sheet title, and messages shorten it.
  • Warm-ups are counted apart, as in the app: two warm-ups in front, and the first work set still reads Set 1. Last time is matched to work sets only.
  • Messages appear just above the bottom controls, never over the clock, and run to two lines at most.
  • Typing a number: tapping any big number opens a pad with ± buttons. Nothing changes until you tap Next or Done, and Cancel leaves it as it was.

What the real app supports

  • Four layouts already: Cards, List, Compact and Focus. A is close to Focus, B to Cards and C to List, so each direction is a restyle of something people already have, not a new way of working.
  • Logging weight × reps with steppers or by typing. A tick logs a set, and ticking it again undoes it. Weight changes carry to later sets.
  • Last time or best set (the reference line can switch), plus the progression reason and rule (Linear, Double progression and others), all quoted here word for word.
  • Rest timer per exercise, with −15 / +15, pause, skip and a Ready state. Plate loading ("each side"), dumbbells, bodyweight and warm-up sets.
  • Add, swap and remove exercises (remove asks first), move up and down, exercise and session notes, and exercise history.
  • Finish sheet: Finish and save, or Discard workout, then a confirmation. It's the only way to discard, as here.
  • Weigh-in before the workout ("Quick check-in", which can be turned off in Settings). Minimize keeps the workout running, and the centre tab shows the time.
  • The exercise animation with the Full / Small / Hidden setting (Settings → During a workout), and how-to steps for every library exercise. The steps here are the app's own, word for word. The figure is a stand-in for the app's real clips.
  • Not shown here, but the app has them: supersets, drop sets and bursts, per-side sets, timed holds, cardio, effort (RIR/RPE) and Apple Watch logging. Each needs its own small state later.

  • New for the appThe clock waits for the first set ("Not started"). The app starts it when the workout begins.
