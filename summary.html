Workout summary

What you see right after Finish and save. It replaces today's "Workout complete!" pop-up. This is where the big streak celebration lives (Today only does the small 6 → 7 roll). Everything shown is something openGym already works out, except the items marked New below. Streak and records are white; the routine color is only the small dot next to the routine name. Pick a situation to see it in all three directions. Pick the same one again to replay the celebration. On the phones, try Done, Add a progress photo, and in B tap an exercise.

Mix

  • What it isA's big moment on top and B's compact exercise list below. On a plain or short day, the moment is a real win: "5 of 6 lifts up on last time".
  • What makes it upliftingVolume shows "+260 vs last". The streak shows how close you are to your best ("3 more weeks to beat your best of 9"). Above Done, there's the next step up ("Next time: Bench press 75 kg × 5"). The check pops in, the numbers count up and the up-arrows pop in as you scroll to the list, once per visit.
  • Honest by designA win is shown only when the numbers back it up. There's no volume comparison on a short day or when an exercise is new, no "next time" line when nothing steps up, and a down day falls back to a calm "Nice work".
Direction A

Moment

  • What it isOne big moment fills the top: the streak with its flame and the number rolling up, or your new records, or a calm "Nice work" on a normal day. The numbers and every exercise scroll underneath.
  • StrengthIt feels like a finish line. The celebration is impossible to miss, and the details are still one scroll away, in the order the app shows them today.
  • RiskOn a normal day the top half is mostly a pat on the back. People who want their numbers have to scroll past it every time.
Direction B

Scoreboard

  • What it isThe workout first: its name, three big numbers, then a dark card with a white flame and number when the streak moves. Exercises are a compact two-line list (name, then today's sets); tap one to see last time and next time.
  • StrengthThe most information on the first screen, and the quickest to read. A plain day looks plain, so the streak card really stands out when it appears.
  • RiskThe celebration is a card, not a moment. Records sit lower down, and long exercise names wrap to two lines in the list.
Direction C

Story

  • What it isFull-screen pages you swipe through, one moment per page: the streak, then records, then the numbers, then the exercises. Only the moments that really happened get a page.
  • StrengthEach win gets the whole screen, at the biggest type in the app. Done works from any page, so nobody is forced through it.
  • RiskMore taps to reach the details, and swiping sideways is a new gesture for openGym. Pages are easy to miss.

Same in every direction

  • One solid button: Done, in white. Close (X) does the same, as in the app: both go to Home, and the workout is already saved.
  • Celebrate what really happened, in order: a streak that moved, then records, then a short day, otherwise a calm "Nice work". There's no streak fanfare on a day that didn't change it.
  • White for wins. Flame, records, the "up" arrows and the trained muscles are white. "Down on last time" is a grey arrow, never red or orange (orange is Legs).
  • No guilt. A short workout says it counts. Exercises not started are mentioned once, as staying in the routine for next time.
  • Stats: duration, volume and work sets, with warm-ups as the small line. The PR count tile from the app is dropped, because the records list already says it.
  • The streak strip draws only the current run: one white tick per week, this week's the tall one. No missed weeks are drawn.
  • Reduced motion: no roll, glow or slide; the new number simply shows.

What the real app supports

  • Already on today's summary: duration, volume, "22 sets · 20 work", new PRs (heaviest weight), best estimated 1-rep max, "What you just trained" body map, last time / next time with up, same or down, cardio, "New in this workout: Add it to Push A", adding photos and clips, Close and one button ("Nice!").
  • Partial workouts: exercises with no ticked sets are dropped when you finish; the routine keeps them.
  • New for the appStreak on the summary. The app already works out the week streak (Home, widgets) and the longest run of weeks (the report), but the summary doesn't show either. The "kept", "new best" and "week 1" moments need the summary to compare the streak before and after this workout.
  • New for the app"was 72.5 kg" next to a record. The app knows the old best (it's what it compares against) but only says "New PR: Bench press".
  • New for the appFirst workout ever. Today the app counts every lift of a first workout as a PR (there's nothing to beat). The design shows "First time" instead, so the records stay meaningful.
  • New for the appWording and colors: "Done" instead of "Nice!", "down" in grey instead of orange, "Next time" in white instead of the accent color.
  • New for the appDirection C's pages are a new pattern; the content on them is all existing.
