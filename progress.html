Progress: three ways in, plus a mix

The fourth tab in the capsule. Today the app calls it Stats ("Progress & history") and stacks a dozen grey cards. Each direction below shows the same history: 56 workouts since 6 Jul, a 9-week run, a week off, then the current 7-week streak; Bench press at 72.5 kg (the same sessions as the Exercises screen); body weight as weekly averages with the 78.0 kg goal dashed, exactly as on Today. Switch the situation to see every direction in each case. Every phone is live: tap workouts, records, exercises and the Log button.

Direction A

Overview

  • What it isOne scroll with every topic once, in the order you'll check them: three totals, then Strength, Body weight, Muscles this week, Records and Recent workouts. Each section opens its detail in a sheet.
  • StrengthClosest to what the app has today, minus the grey cards. Nothing is more than one swipe away, and the big lifts are one tap each.

Direction B

One at a time

  • What it isA switch at the top: Lifts · Weight · Muscles · History. Each topic gets the whole screen: a bigger chart, its own period, the last five workouts, the full week list.
  • StrengthCalm and deep. Long exercise names get a full-width selector with two lines. Room for every chart the app has without crowding.
  • RiskThree of the four topics are hidden at any time, so a new record or a missed muscle can go unseen. It's one more tap to see what changed.
Direction C

Logbook

  • What it isHistory first. Each week is a chapter: its workouts, how much you lifted, the records you set and that week's average weight. Weeks off are named kindly. Filter by routine at the top.
  • StrengthTells the story of the habit, and puts weight next to the training that caused it. Charts are one tap away: any workout, then any exercise.
  • RiskNo chart on the first screen, so "am I getting stronger?" takes two taps. Muscles aren't here at all; they'd need to move to Exercises or a workout's sheet.

Try on any phone

  • Tap a workout for its sheet: time, sets, tonnes, records, every exercise with its sets. Tap an exercise for its line. Delete workout asks first, then the totals, charts and lists update.
  • Exercise picker (A: "All 17", B: the selector): type to search. Picking one redraws the chart.
  • Top set / Est. max and 1M · 3M · 1Y · All redraw the chart and the "Up … since" line.
  • Log opens the weigh-in sheet. Today is already logged, so it edits today's number; the weekly column and average update.
  • Close any sheet with X, Escape or a tap outside. B's topic switch also works with the arrow keys.

Same in all three

  • No grey cards, no lime. Sections sit on black with a hairline, like Today. Charts, records and the "now" column are white.
  • Color only marks a routine: the dot on each workout row, the filled days on the calendar, the routine filter. A running workout is a white pulsing ring.
  • Body weight matches Today's chosen C: weekly columns, this week white, goal dashed, the same sentences ("Losing about 0.4 kg a week, on track for your goal").
  • Records are white trophies and filled white dots on a lift's line. The app already marks a workout's records (PRs).
  • Encouragement, never guilt. "A week off. Rest counts too." Untrained muscles are an invitation (tap for exercises), not a warning.
  • Long names never break: one line with … in rows and chips, two lines in the selector and sheet titles. Try "Long names".
  • The streak stays on Today. Progress doesn't repeat the 7; C shows the best run (9) as part of the story.

What the real app supports

  • Totals: workouts, this month, lifted in total. Activity for 12 months (B and C show it as a month calendar instead).
  • Exercise progress with a searchable picker, Top set, Est. 1RM and 1M/3M/1Y/All. Also Per set, Effort, Max reps and a body-weight view, not drawn here; B has room for them as more switch options.
  • Muscle balance by sets for Week (done of planned), 30 and 90 days, plus "Not trained" muscles that open their exercises. Fatigue and Strength maps exist too.
  • Body weight goal, Log, weekly rate and "All weigh-ins" grouped by week. History with every workout, PR counts, Log a past workout and Delete with a confirmation.
  • Not placed yet: Structural balance, Progress photos, Body measurements, Effort and the Performance report. They need a row on this tab or a move.

New for the app

  • NewRecords list: "Records · last 30 days" and "Best lifts" across all exercises. The app knows each workout's records but has no list of them.
  • NewWeeks as chapters (C): each week's tonnes, records and weight average, and the "weeks off" notes.
  • NewRoutine filter on history (C).
