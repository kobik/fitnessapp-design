Exercises: the library

The fifth tab. It's where you look up any of the app's 5,632 exercises, see how to do one, check your own history and best on it, put it in a routine, or make your own exercise. Three directions, one shared exercise sheet. Pick a situation to switch every phone at once. Everything is live: type in the search, tap muscles and equipment, open an exercise, star it, add it to a routine, create or delete your own (deleting asks first). The mock holds 47 of the app's 5,632 exercises, so its counts are smaller than the real ones.

Situation

Direction A

Search first

  • What it isThe app's list, cleaned up. A search field at the top with a live count, a row of muscle chips, and equipment chips once you've narrowed things down. Favourites come first, then everything A to Z, each with your best on the right.
  • StrengthClosest to today's app, so nothing to relearn. Fastest when you know what you're looking for: two or three letters and it's there.
  • RiskBrowsing a 5,600-item list is a wall of rows. People who don't know the name of what they want get little help.
Direction B

Body map

  • What it isA big front and back figure, the same one as on Home. Tap a muscle (or pick it from the list underneath) and it lights up white, then you see what trains it: mainly first, then exercises where it helps. Search hides behind a button.
  • StrengthAnswers "what can I do for my shoulders?" in one tap. It folds the app's separate "By muscle" page into the tab and matches the Home look.
  • RiskSearching by name takes one more tap. The figure is small for fingers, so the list of muscles below has to stay as the dependable way in.
Direction C

Yours first

  • What it isStarts with what you already do: the exercises in your plan, one routine at a time (Push A, Pull A, Legs) with your best, then your own exercises, then muscles to browse. Search results are split into "Yours" and "From the library".
  • StrengthMost visits are about an exercise you already do ("what did I bench last week?"). This puts that one tap away.
  • RiskIt repeats some of the Plan tab. A new user sees an empty top section, so the empty text has to do real work (try "New user").

Same in all three

  • One exercise sheet. The animation at the top, then the name, main muscle and helpers, a small body map (main muscle white, helpers light grey), how-to steps, your history, and one solid button: Add to a routine.
  • Animations: list rows show a still of each exercise and the sheet plays the clip, as in the app. The mock draws a stand-in figure; the app shows its own clip. Your own exercises show your photo or video, or "No photo or video yet".
  • History lives in the sheet. Your best and how many sessions, a white line of your working weight, then your sessions with PRs, newest first. "Show more" opens up to the last 6, and "All 15 sessions in Progress" leads on from there. Bench press: 72.5 kg best, 15 sessions since July, last on 21 Oct (today's Push A isn't done yet).
  • Never done reads "You haven't done this one yet" and says what will appear there, without any guilt.
  • Muscles are white, never a routine color. Color only appears next to Push A, Pull A and Legs, where it marks the routine.
  • No dead ends. No exact match shows close matches, a "Search all muscles" button when a filter is on, and "Create 'sissy squat'" with the name already filled in. Typos are forgiven ("lateral rasie" finds Lateral raise).
  • Long names wrap to two lines in lists and in full on the sheet. Try "Long names", then open the landmine one.
  • Your own exercises can be edited and deleted. Deleting asks first, and closing an unsaved new exercise asks before throwing it away. Until it can save, the form says what's missing.
  • The + on every row goes straight to "Add to a routine", like the app's Plan button. A routine that already has the exercise shows a check and "Already in", and isn't a choice. Picking one says "Added to Pull A. Set its sets and weight in Plan."
  • A muscle filter never hides. In B and C, searching while a muscle is picked shows a removable "Only Shoulders ×" chip above the results.
  • Equipment is optional for your own exercises. Leave it empty and none is shown.

What the real app supports

  • Search with a live count, typo tolerance, "yours first" while searching, and "No exact match. These come close".
  • Filters: body part, type of exercise (strength, stretching, cardio and more) and equipment. Equipment choices come from what's left, so they never lead to nothing. There's also an equipment profile ("Showing what you have in Home gym").
  • Favourites (star), which float to the top. A list or card view switch.
  • A separate "By muscle" page with a body map, reached from a button on the list.
  • The exercise sheet: animation, tags, description, how-to steps, best and last sets, a History sheet with a chart and sessions, an estimated 1-rep max calculator, plate loading and weight stack settings, alternate names, and "Suggest a fix".
  • Custom exercises: name and body part, with optional equipment, muscles, photo or video, a link and a note. Edit and delete (with confirmation).
  • Add to my plan: pick a routine or a new one, then sets, reps and weight.

New for the app

  • Muscle chips on the main list (A) in place of body parts like "upper legs". The app only filters by muscle on its separate page.
  • The body map as the tab's front page (B), with "mainly" and "also works" groups.
  • "In your plan" by routine and "Your own" as sections (C), and search results split into Yours and Library.
  • History inside the exercise sheet instead of a separate History sheet, showing the best and the session count, with older sessions handed over to Progress ("All 15 sessions in Progress").
  • The muscle body map on the exercise sheet, under the animation.
  • "Search all muscles" on an empty filtered search, and the "In Push A" line on the sheet.
  • Left out of the mock but kept: the 1-rep max calculator, plate and stack settings, alternate names, type of exercise, the equipment profile and the card view. They'd sit lower on the sheet or in the filters, unchanged.
