openGym redesign · Exercise animation · iPhone

Where you see the exercise animation

The real app plays a short clip for each exercise. These mocks can’t use those clips, so a looping white line figure stands in, tagged Animation. It shows where and how big the clip appears, not what it looks like. It stops moving when the phone is set to reduce motion. Every phone works: tap a thumbnail to open the full view.

A1

Workout A · Full

  • What it isThe animation loops at the top of the one-set screen. The weight, reps and Log stay at the bottom where your thumb is.
  • StrengthYou see the form while you set the weight, with no tap.
  • RiskTakes the top of the screen. On small phones, less room for the exercise name.
A2

Workout A · Small

  • What it isA small still beside the exercise name. Tap it for the full view.
  • StrengthCalm screen for people who know the moves.
  • RiskThe form is one tap away, so beginners may skip it.
A3

Full view (tap to open)

  • What it isThe animation large, with the app’s own how-to steps under it. Close with X, Escape or a tap outside.
  • StrengthOne place to learn a move, the same from every screen.
  • RiskCovers the workout, so it must stay one tap to close.
A4

Workout A · Resting

  • What it isWhile the rest timer runs, the animation shows the next exercise, marked “Up next”. New for the app.
  • StrengthUses the rest time to get ready for what’s next.
  • RiskThe picture changes under you, so the “Up next” label must be clear.
C

Workout C · Thumbnail

  • What it isIn the whole-session list, only the current exercise has a thumbnail. Tap for the full view.
  • StrengthKeeps the busy list readable.
  • RiskSmall, so it’s easy to miss.
X

Exercises · Exercise sheet

  • What it isThe animation large at the top of the exercise, the muscle map smaller below. List rows show a still.
  • StrengthMatches what people expect from an exercise library.
  • RiskThe muscle map moves down and gets smaller.
P

Plan · Routine editor

  • What it isA small still beside each exercise in a routine. Tap it for the how-to sheet.
  • StrengthHelps when building a routine with new exercises.
  • RiskOrder numbers were removed to keep room for long names.

Same everywhere

  • One full view. Every thumbnail opens the same big animation with the how-to steps.
  • White and grey only. The animation never takes a routine color.
  • Follows the app’s setting. The app already has Full / Small / Hidden for workout animations. Full and Small are shown here; Hidden shows nothing.
  • Not on Summary or Progress. Those screens are about results.

What the real app supports

  • Animations for its built-in exercises, downloaded to the phone, with a still-only option to save battery.
  • Your own exercises have no clip. They show “No photo or video yet”.
  • New for the app: the “Up next” animation during rest, and stills in the Plan editor.

The stand-in movements

The stand-in has a handful of basic movements, picked by the exercise name. The real clips replace all of them.
