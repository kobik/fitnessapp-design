Photo hero

Your Push photo in two layouts. Full-bleed: the photo fills the top of the screen and the workout name, muscles and Start sit on the dark bottom of the image. Framed: the photo is a tall rounded card and the title sits on black below it. Both drift very slowly (a 22-second push-in) and stay still when reduced motion is on. Only the hero is being judged here; everything below it is unchanged from the last round.

How it scales to the real app

  • One photo per muscle focus, not per workout: Push, Pull, Legs, Shoulders, Arms, Core, Full body, Cardio, plus a rest-day photo. Custom routines pick theirs by main muscle group.
  • About 110 KB each as JPG (your Push photo converted from 680 KB), so all of them ship inside the app and work offline.
  • The rim light in each photo matches its routine color, so photos and UI read as one system.
