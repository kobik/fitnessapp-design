Body weight: week by week

Chosen: C, week by week. A and B stay on the board for reference. The last section on Today, under the week. Each phone opens scrolled to the bottom, with the capsule over it. The big number is this week's average, the number openGym already uses, so one heavy morning doesn't swing it. Switch the situation to see each direction in every case. The last three are goal moments: they draw only what the app sends (table below). Click Goal reached (moment) again to replay it. Tap Log on any phone to try the weigh-in sheet.

Direction A

Big number

  • What it isThis week's average at hero size, then a sentence on the direction, then a white trend line across the full width with your goal as a dashed line.
  • StrengthIt has the same energy as the hero and reads from arm's length. The line shows the whole month at a glance.

Direction B

Goal track

  • What it isOne bar from where you started to your goal, with you on it, plus how much you've done so far and your weekly pace as a small tag.
  • StrengthIt reads the same for losing or gaining, and it celebrates progress made, not just distance left.
  • RiskIt doesn't show day-to-day ups and downs. With no goal set, it has the least to show.
Direction C · Chosen

Week by week

  • What it isOne column per week with its average written on top. This week is white, and your goal is a dashed line.
  • StrengthIt matches the week strip above it and shows the habit as well as the direction. The numbers are all readable.
  • RiskChanges of a few hundred grams make columns that look almost equal. The exact numbers on top carry the meaning.

Goal moments: what the app provides

The screen doesn't decide any of this. The app sends one goal object with the fields below. If it sends nothing, the section shows no moment, band or checks, just the normal weekly view.

FieldMeaningUsed for
reachedThe goal is reached, judged on the weekly averageSolid white goal line, "Holding range" line
celebrateTrue only until the moment has been shown once. The app clears it afterwards."Goal reached" headline, the column rising
change, sincekg up or down since the first weigh-in, and that date"You did it. 1.5 kg down since 29 Sep."
rangeHolding range, ± kg around the goalThe soft band behind the columns
holdWeeksWeeks in a row inside the range since reaching the goal"3 weeks holding at your goal"
weekInFor each week shown: inside the range or notThe white check under each column

New for the app: openGym has a goal weight today, but no holding range, no "moment shown" flag and no holding count. All three would need adding. ±1 kg is a proposed default.

What the real app supports

  • A goal weight that can be above or below you. The app works out gain or lose from it.
  • Weekly averages and a weekly pace. The pace is a straight line through the last 3 weeks of weigh-ins, and it needs at least 4 of them, as in the mock.
  • kg or lb for the whole profile. The mock shows kg only.
  • A weigh-in prompt before each workout (users can turn it off). The Log button here may duplicate it, which is worth deciding.
  • Not built yet: "Set a goal" here opens a placeholder. The app's goal sheet lives elsewhere today.
