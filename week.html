The week: streak options

The week is the marked Mon–Sun strip. On top sits the streak, three ways, each with its own voice. The streak follows openGym's real rule: weeks in a row with at least one workout. Switch the streak situation to read every line of copy in place; the full set is in the table below.

Week

Streak situations change only the streak. Streak kept plays the one-time moment; click it again to replay.

Streak A

Flame card

  • What it isA card with a white flame, the big number, the last 12 weeks as small bars, and one warm sentence.
  • StrengthSays the most: the number, the history and a real reason to keep going, in plain words.
  • RiskThe only card left on the screen; it can feel like a widget.
Streak B

Poster

  • What it isNo card. The number at hero size with a flame, and a short caps headline beside it, like the hero title.
  • StrengthThe most energy; it matches the photo hero and reads from arm's length.
  • RiskBig type for a small fact; the hype headlines can get old if you see them daily.
Streak C

Chain

  • What it isOne link per week in a row, running toward your best. Your best and the next record are marked on the chain.
  • StrengthShows the goal, not just the count: you can see how close the next record is.
  • RiskMore abstract; the facts-only caption is the least warm of the three.

All the copy

One row per situation. Coach is warm and full-sentence (card). Hype is a caps headline plus a line (poster). Stats is just the facts (chain). Any voice can go on any look.

Same in all three

  • White, not lime. Color still only marks routines.
  • Encouraging, never guilt. No "you lost it", no countdown alarms. A missed week just starts week 1 again.
  • Streak kept plays once: the first time Today is opened after the week's first workout. The number rolls 6 → 7 and the flame lights. Later workouts that week don't replay it. The bigger celebration belongs on the workout summary screen (a later part).
  • Honest numbers. Every line comes from the streak, the best run and the current week.
